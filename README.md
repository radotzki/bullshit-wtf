# Bullshit: the free bluffing trivia party game

**Play at [bullshit.wtf](https://bullshit.wtf/?via=github)**, free, in the browser, with no app to install.

A trivia game where the best liar wins. A strange true fact appears with a blank, and everyone writes
a lie on their own phone that sounds true enough to fool the others. All the answers appear together,
the lies and the truth, and everyone picks the one they believe. Find the truth and you score; every
friend who picks your lie scores for you too. Up to 12 players, in English or Hebrew, with the
questions and scores on a TV or laptop if you like.
