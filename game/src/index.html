Bullshit: a free party game for phones

Everyone plays on their own phone, in the same room, in the browser. A strange true fact appears with a blank, everyone writes a lie to fill it, and then everyone tries to find the truth among all the answers. Fool a friend and you score. Find the truth and you score. Free, no download, no sign-up, up to 12 players, with questions in English or Hebrew.

How it works

  1. One person creates a game and gets a four-letter PIN.
  2. Everyone else joins from their own phone with the PIN.
  3. Write a lie, then find the truth. 5, 7 or 9 questions, and the points grow each round.

For a bigger group, put the questions and the scores on a TV or laptop with Present Game.
